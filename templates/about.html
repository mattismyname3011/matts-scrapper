About Media Scraper

A lightweight tool for processing publicly exposed download links from supported pages.

What is Media Scraper?

Media Scraper is a web-based utility that accepts a supported page URL and extracts publicly exposed download links from that page.

Direct URL approach

Instead of searching an entire website, Media Scraper works with a URL supplied by the user. This keeps the process simple and minimizes unnecessary requests.

Responsible use

The application does not attempt to bypass authentication, CAPTCHA, DRM, paywalls, or other access controls.

Users are responsible for complying with applicable laws and the terms of the websites they access.
